Idea

A 2×2 data grid with large outer corners reads as a speech bubble. The bottom-right corner is left sharp. That gives the bubble its tail and the "Q" its stroke. The violet cell is the answer the data returns.

Rules

Use violet on one cell only. Leave clear space of at least one tile width (29u) around the mark. In single-colour use, all four cells take the same colour. The sharp corner keeps the shape readable.
